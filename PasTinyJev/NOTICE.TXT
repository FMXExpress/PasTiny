PasTinyJev for real-mode DOS, built on FMXExpress/PasTiny and PasTinyMoE.
The network design, the original Tiny program and GENERAL.BIN (the unchanged
TINY 2.1 model) are from WollyMonjac/Tiny-2.1.
TRIAGE.BIN was trained from that model on synthetic support messages.
The request format follows TypeSafe's Jev and razorback16/openjev; this
project is not affiliated with or endorsed by either.
