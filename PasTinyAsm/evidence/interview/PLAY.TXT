The three programs run under DOS with keys from a file.

CAVE.COM, keys e b e o:
  you are in a dark cave
  press n for north or e for east
  you find a locked door
  press o for open or b for back
  you walk back
  you are in a dark cave
  press n for north or e for east
  you find a locked door
  press o for open or b for back
  the door opens and you are free

CAVE.COM, keys x n:
  you are in a dark cave
  press n for north or e for east
  you are in a dark cave
  press n for north or e for east
  you fall into a pit
  game over

MENU.COM, keys 1 x 2 q:
  press 1 for a beep
  press 2 for a siren
  press q to quit
  that is not on the menu
  press 1 for a beep
  press 2 for a siren
  press q to quit
  that is not on the menu
  press 1 for a beep
  press 2 for a siren
  press q to quit
  that is not on the menu
  press 1 for a beep
  press 2 for a siren
  press q to quit
  goodbye

HOUSE.COM, keys w x w c:
  you stand in front of an old house
  press d for the door or w for the window
  you climb in through the window
  there is a chest here
  press c to open the chest
  you leave the chest alone and climb out again
  nothing happens
  you stand in front of an old house
  press d for the door or w for the window
  you climb in through the window
  there is a chest here
  press c to open the chest
  the chest is full of gold
  you win

HOUSE.COM, keys d:
  you stand in front of an old house
  press d for the door or w for the window
  the door creaks open
  a ghost is waiting inside
  you run away screaming
